Para revocarle el acceso a Walk Alone: Health Connect → Permisos de aplicaciones → Walk Alone → quitar todos los permisos.

Delete your account and data

Walk Alone · com.alonecompany.walkalone · Updated August 18, 2026

You can delete your Walk Alone account and all associated data at any time. There are two ways.

Option 1 — In the app

  1. Open Walk Alone and sign in.
  2. Go to the Profile tab.
  3. Tap Delete account.
  4. Confirm.

Deletion is immediate.

Option 2 — By email

Write to ianlopezdev@gmail.com from the email address associated with your account, requesting deletion. We respond within 30 days.

What gets deleted

DataWhat happens
Account and profile data (first name, last name, date of birth, height, weight, email)Permanently deleted
All recorded activities (date, duration, distance, type, steps, calories, elevation gain)Permanently deleted
All GPS coordinates from your routesPermanently deleted
Heart rate and oxygen saturation (SpO₂)Permanently deleted
No data is retained. Deletion is complete and irreversible: there is no retention period afterwards, no backups kept, and no anonymized data stored. Once your account is deleted, it cannot be recovered, and neither can your activity history.

Health Connect data

Walk Alone only reads from Health Connect; it never modifies or deletes anything there. Data living in Health Connect belongs to that app and remains after you delete your Walk Alone account. To manage it, open the Health Connect app on your device.

To revoke Walk Alone's access: Health Connect → App permissions → Walk Alone → remove all permissions.
